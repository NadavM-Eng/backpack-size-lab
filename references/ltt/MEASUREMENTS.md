## Colour and material
- Fabric: black ballistic nylon, **#1b1c1e**, matte (roughness about 0.75), with a visible
  basket weave.
- Bottom panel: about **#2a2b2e**, smoother and slightly glossy (roughness about 0.45).
- Zips: coated, glossy black (roughness about 0.3).
- Carabiner pulls and buckles: light-grey titanium **#a7a9ac**, metallic, roughness about 0.35.
