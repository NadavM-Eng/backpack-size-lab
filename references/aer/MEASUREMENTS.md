# Aer City Pack Pro 2 (20 L and 24 L): measurements taken from the photos

Published sizes: 20 L = 44.5 H × 30.5 W × 18 D cm; 24 L = 46 H × 30.5 W × 18 D cm.
Silhouettes were measured by thresholding the light-grey studio background (#f2f2f2, |Δ| > 40)
and reading row widths / runs (scripts in the session scratchpad; numbers below are what they
printed). "Viewer's left" = as seen from the front = -X in the model (front = -Y in Blender).

## Scale
- 20L_front: body width at mid-height 293 px; handle top y 58 → base y 474 (421 px with the
  handle). 421/44.5 = 9.46 px/cm → width 31 cm, so ~**9.5 px/cm** (the 30.5 cm width and the
  44.5 cm height that includes the handle agree within 2 %).
- Body top (front-panel dome, handle excluded) y ≈ 74 → body height ≈ 400 px = **42.0 cm**; the
  handle adds ≈ 2.5 cm (44.5 total).
- 24L_front: width 229 px; handle top y 36, body top y ≈ 60, base y 374 → 7.5 px/cm,
  body ≈ 314 px = **43.5 cm** (+1.5 cm, matching the published +1.5 cm).
- H/W including the handle: 20 L 1.437, 24 L 1.48 (published ratios 1.459 / 1.508).

- Back face (y 301–304 px in 20L_sideright) is vertical; the front face leans back toward the
  base (front edge x 105 px at 0.78 H → 149 px at the base): a **wedge**, deepest at ~0.8 H,
  with a big rounded front-top corner (the dome) and a tight back-top corner (~3 cm).
- Absolute: 20 L max ≈ 0.496 × 42 = 20.8 cm, 24 L max ≈ 0.46 × 43.5 = 20.0 cm. Both read ~10 %
  deeper than the published 18 cm (studio bags are stuffed; mesh back padding included), so
  the model applies ×0.90 → **≈ 18.7 / 18.0 cm** at the fullest, ~15 cm at the base.
- **How the 24 L differs**: +1.5 cm taller body, same width, practically the same depth (the
  extra volume comes from height and the fuller shoulders, not depth). Its front face is closer
  to vertical (less wedge: 0.43–0.46 H from 0.1 to 0.85 H vs 0.40–0.50 on the 20 L) and the lower
  part is fuller.

## Front panel (20L_front, contrast-enhanced crop)
- Front-panel seam (piping) inset ~3 cm from the silhouette at the sides, ~2.4 cm below the top.
- **Front-pocket zip**: horizontal at y ≈ 240 px → z ≈ 24.6 cm = **0.585 H**, across the panel
  (x −11.3…+12.6 cm), slight smile (ends ~0.7 cm higher). Coated zip with a glossy welt, short
  coated **garage at the viewer's left end**, long flat pull hanging ~8 cm (y 245→320 px).
- **Upper darts** (pinched diagonal folds) at the upper panel corners: from (±10.6, 0.873 H)
  to (±8.3, 0.836 H), ~2.5 cm long, running inward-down.
- **Lower V-darts**: from (±8.9, 0.148 H) up-inward to (±4.6, 0.205 H), ~5 cm long.
- **Top quick-access zip** across the top just behind the front panel (visible as a curved line
  over the top in 20L_front / 20L_top_quickaccess_handle).

## Sides (20L_sideright, enhanced crop; fractions are of the depth measured from the back)

## Back and harness (20L_back, 20L_NN_straps_reflective)
- Top handle: padded webbing, ~11.5 cm span, at the top-back edge, rises ~2.5 cm.
- Straps start near the top centre (x ≈ ±4.5 cm), ~7 cm wide at the top tapering to ~6 cm,
  padded part ends at y ≈ 350 px → z ≈ 13 cm (0.30 H); webbing → ladder lock → bottom corners.
  In side view they bow out ~7–9 cm from the back (studio).
- **Reflective band** ~2 cm wide across each strap near the top, diagonal (inner edge higher).
- D-ring on each strap face at ~0.6 H; sternum strap on short rails, sitting at ~0.4 H.
- Back panel: upper mesh blocks (0.53–0.89 H), lower perforated pads (0.07–0.455 H), central
  channel. **Luggage pass-through** across the full back at z ≈ 20.5 cm (0.465–0.515 H).

## Colour / material
- 1680D ballistic nylon, near-black **#18191b**, matte with a tight visible weave (roughness ~0.6).
- Coated zips/garages and the coated strip: same hue, glossy (roughness ~0.2).
- Webbing black; reflective bands mid grey, semi-gloss; D-rings gunmetal.
