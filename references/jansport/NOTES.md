# JanSport SuperBreak (classic school backpack) — 12 reference photos
This is the iconic JanSport SuperBreak / SuperBreak One: a soft, unstructured, rounded "school bag" shape
(the classic JanSport silhouette: rounded top, tall body, one big front utility pocket with a curved top
edge and a U-shaped zip, the red/blue "JANSPORT" woven label patch above the front pocket, a web haul
loop on top, simple foam-padded shoulder straps with plain webbing + ladder adjusters at the bottom,
no sternum strap, no load lifters, no back padding beyond a plain back panel, 600D polyester, soft and
slouchy — it holds its shape only from contents).
- walmart_*: studio shots, black. walmart_front_black_bottle_pocket shows the current version with a
  stretch side water-bottle pocket on one side (simons photos show it too). MODEL THE BLACK BAG WITH THE
  SIDE BOTTLE POCKET.
- NN_*: Nomads Nation real-world review shots of a RED older SuperBreak (no bottle pocket) — use for
  shape, front pocket, straps, back panel, stitching; ignore colour.
- simons_*: orange colourway studio shots incl. back/side with the bottle pocket and the strap label.
Published: ~25–26 L, ~0.3–0.34 kg, 600D polyester. Retailer-listed size: 45 H × 40 W × 15 D cm (Simons,
probably measured flat/empty — width 40 is the flat width); JanSport has commonly listed ~16.7 × 13 × 8.5 in
(42.4 H × 33 W × 21.6 D cm) when filled. Reconcile from photo proportions: model it moderately filled.
