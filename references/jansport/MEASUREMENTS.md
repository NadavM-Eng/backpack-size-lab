- It is a **tombstone, widest low down**. Max width is 31.5 cm over z 4–17, and it narrows steadily to
  about 26 cm at z 30 (a soft taper, not straight sides). The top is a broad dome: the curve
  starts about 6 cm below the top and has no flat section.
- The bottom corners are tight, radius about 2.5 cm. The base is flat and sags slightly.
- Model **W = 32 cm** (between the 31.5 measured here and the 33 published flat width) and **H = 42.5 cm**.

## Side profile / depth (walmart_34_black and the published sizes)
- The side gusset reads about 0.31 of the front width at about 30° rotation, so the **main body is
  about 15 cm deep** at the base.
- Depth tapers to about 9–10 cm near the top, because the front panel rolls back over the dome.
  The back is flat-ish with no padding.
- The front pocket adds about **4–5 cm** (its lid bulges most). Overall with the straps is about 23–24 cm.

## Front utility pocket (simons 3.27 px/cm; black 5.3 px/cm agrees within 0.5 cm)
- Outline: **24 cm wide** (x ±12), top edge at **z ≈ 25.3**, bottom edge at **z ≈ 4.3**.
  It is a rounded rectangle with corners of about 3 cm, and the top corners are rounder (about 4 cm).
  It sits inside the body silhouette by about 3.5–4 cm at the sides.
- **Lid band** (gusseted, puffy) runs from the top edge z 25.3 down to the **zip at z ≈ 20.3**.
- **Zip path is an inverted U.** It runs horizontally across at z 20.3, x ±10.5, rounds the corners
  (r ≈ 2.5) and runs down both sides to **z ≈ 14.5**. Zip ends sit about 1.3 cm inside the pocket edge.
- Pull: a black **cord loop** on a slider parked at the right (+X) top corner of the zip, hanging down about 6 cm
  (NN_front_pocket_closeup, black front).
- The pocket body below the zip is soft and sags. Horizontal slump creases run across its lower third
  (NN closeup), and the lower front of the main body puffs out below the pocket.

## Label patch
- Navy (#1d2a55) woven rectangle with a red outline, lettered "JANSPORT" in red with a white outline.
  **9.5 × 5.0 cm**, centred at **x 0, z ≈ 33.4** (simons z 33.6, black z 33.2), with square-ish corners.

## Haul loop (top)
- Black web loop, **2.5 cm webbing**, outer width about **7.5 cm**, standing about **7.5 cm** above the top.
  It is stitched just behind the top centre (about 65 % of the depth back from the front).

## Main zip
- A two-way zip runs over the top in the gusset, about 40 % of the depth back from the front, and down both
  sides to **z ≈ 16** (walmart_34). Two black cord pulls are parked near the upper right
  (black front, x 295 px).

## Shoulder straps (NN_back_straps_red, NN_strap_padding_topdown, simons_back)
- Thin foam straps, **6 cm wide × about 1.5 cm**, with no mesh and a plain polyester cover. They leave the back
  close together at the top centre (inner edges about ±1.5 cm, z ≈ 40), just behind the haul loop.
- The padded part runs to about z 12. From there, **2.5 cm webbing** runs through a plastic ladder lock
  (about z 8–9) down to a tab at the bottom corners (z ≈ 1.5, x ≈ ±13). Webbing **tails dangle** 10–15 cm
  below the ladder locks.
- The back panel is the same plain fabric with no padding. A thin 0.3 cm "back_pad" panel is used only
  so the website can align the bags.

## Bottle pocket (-X side, current version)
- Stretch polyester, **elastic top at z ≈ 19–20**, occupying the front about 70 % of the side.
  It bulges about 3–4 cm with a bottle (the black front shows it reaching to x = -18.5 at the base).

## Fabric
- 600D polyester: a fine, dull weave. There are soft wrinkles at the lower front, around the pocket lid and
  over the top dome, with mild sag at the base.
