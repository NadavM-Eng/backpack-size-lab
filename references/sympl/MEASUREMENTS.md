# Sympl Commuter Pack 20L (V2.2): measurements taken from the photos

Known size: 46 H × 30 W × 17 D cm. Pixel measurements were taken on the straight-front
studio shot **04** (480×625). The bag is about 285 px wide there, so the scale is about
**9.43 px/cm**. The side shots 20 and 15 and the back shots 14 and 11 confirm it.

## Overall
- Body height without the handle: 117→521 px = **42.8 cm**. The handle arch adds about 3.3 cm,
  so the total is about **46 cm** (the published 46 cm includes the handle).
- Body width: **30 cm** at mid-height (z ≈ 18–26 cm, the widest point).
- Body depth (front panel to the back-panel pads): photos 20 and 15 give depth/height
  ≈ 0.40, so about **17 cm** including about 1.4 cm of moulded pads. Straps hang out a further
  4–6 cm (overall about 22–23 cm with straps).

- The **top corners are elliptical, not circular**: about rx 8 cm × ry 13.5 cm. The flat top
  is about 14 cm wide.
- The **bottom corners** are about rx 5 × ry 7 cm. The base is flat over about ±9 cm.
- The sides are slightly barrel-shaped: about 0.5–0.8 cm narrower at z 8 and z 34 than at
  mid-height. There is no taper.
- Plan-section corner radius (where the front face turns into the side): about **4 cm**. The
  front panel edge is seen about 3.7 cm inside the silhouette.

## Side profile (20, 15)
- Depth is almost constant over the height. The top is fairly flat, with side-profile corner
  radius about **5 cm** at the top and about 2.5–3 cm at the bottom.
- The front face is only gently domed (about 1 cm). The back is flat, plus the moulded pads.

## Front panel
- A clean panel with no pocket outline. Its edge sits about **3.7 cm** in from the silhouette
  at the sides and about **1.3 cm** below the top edge (panel top at z ≈ 41.5).
- **Vertical quick-access zip** on the panel's left edge (x ≈ -11.1 cm), running from
  **z ≈ 38 down to the lower-band seam (z ≈ 7)**. AquaGuard (coated, no visible teeth).
  The slider sits at the top with a **long flat pull tab, about 5 cm × 1.3 cm**, lying down
  along the zip (z 38→33).
- **Tech-caddy zip** along the panel's top edge, from the top of the left zip over the top
  to the right top corner (about z 36 on the right). Its pull is at the top-left.
- The right edge of the panel has piping only, from z 36 down to the band corner.

## Lower band (glossier coated panel)
- Front seam: **horizontal at z ≈ 7.0 cm**, from x ≈ -10 to +11 cm.
- At each end the seam turns into a **chamfer running down and outward** (dx ≈ 2.2, dz ≈ 2.8),
  reaching the silhouette edge at z ≈ 4.3. The panel's side edges meet the seam exactly at the
  chamfer's top corner.
- On the sides the band edge runs back and slightly down (z ≈ 4.3 at the front, 3 at the back),
  and the band wraps the whole base.

## Logo
- Lowercase "sympl studios", tone-on-tone (darker and glossier than the fabric). Centred at
  **x ≈ +0.3, z ≈ 8.3 cm**, about 1.3 cm above the band seam. About **4.7 cm wide** with
  letters about 0.5 cm tall.
- A vertical "sympl studios" label patch on the -X side, low down (26), about 1.6 × 6 cm.
- "sympl studios" printed across the lower part of the shoulder straps (24, 25), and
  "studios" embossed on the lower back pad (12).

## Top handle (04, 20, 23)
- A round padded glossy tube about **2.2 cm** in diameter. The arch's outer span is about
  **14.5 cm** (centreline ±6.4 cm) and it rises about **3.3 cm** above the top surface.
- Seen from the side it sits at about 40 % of the depth measured from the back.

## Side zips and layering (20, 15, 16, 07)
Positions are measured as a fraction of the depth from the back:
- About 0.12: back-panel piping.
- About **0.27: laptop zip**, over the top and down both sides to z ≈ 0.55 H. Its pull is at the top back.
- About **0.55: clamshell zip**, over the top and down both sides to z ≈ 0.1 H. Two pulls near the top corners.
- About **0.80 on the -X side only: vertical side-pocket zip**, z ≈ 0.15 H to 0.78 H, pull at the top
  (07 shows it open with a bottle inside).
- A vertical padded grab strip on the -X side, mid-depth, z ≈ 0.15–0.65 H, about 2.5 cm wide (20, 26).
- Pulls are flat, dark coated-fabric tabs about 4–5 cm × 1 cm.

## Bottle pocket (+X side, the viewer's right) (10, 15, 28)
- Stretch black mesh covering the **front ~75 % of the side depth**, from about 1 cm above the
  base to **z ≈ 0.46 H (about 19.5 cm)**. The top edge is roughly level, with an elastic bound hem
  about 1 cm wide. Empty, it lies almost flat against the side.

## Back and harness (11, 14, 12, 24, 25)
- Moulded pads span x ≈ ±12 cm. The upper block (z 0.53–0.87 H) has a 1.5 cm central channel
  and a cross groove. The lower block (z 0.10–0.42 H) is split into 2×2.
- **Luggage pass-through band**: striped webbing about 23 cm wide, from z 0.43 to 0.51 H
  (about 3.5 cm tall), with three grey stripes.
- **Straps**: they start almost touching at the top centre (centres x ≈ ±3.9, z ≈ 0.89 H) and are
  **7 cm wide at the top, tapering to about 5.5 cm**. They are about 1.8 cm thick and glossy,
  with bound edges. The padded part ends at z ≈ 0.21 H (about 9 cm) at x ≈ ±10. In side view
  they bow out about 5–6 cm from the back at mid-height and about 3 cm at the bottom end.
- A webbing rail runs on the strap face for the sternum strap. The sternum strap and its
  buckle sit at z ≈ 0.62 H.
- Below the padding: 2.5 cm webbing → **ladder lock** at about z 5 cm → webbing to anchors at
  the bottom corners (x ≈ ±12.5, z ≈ 2.5), plus a loose tail.

## Colour and material
- Fabric: near-black **#1c1d1f**, coated satin with soft wrinkles. Roughness about 0.5.
- Lower band and handle: same hue, glossier (roughness about 0.3).
- Mesh: very dark open weave. Webbing: black with grey stripes on the luggage band and the
  strap rails.
