## What I observed (verify against the photos)
- Front silhouette: tall rounded rectangle ("pill / tombstone"); big top corner radius (~8–9 cm), bottom corners ~6 cm; sides nearly straight and parallel. No taper worth modelling.
- Side profile: ~17 cm deep, rounded top, front panel gently domed, back fairly flat. The bag is clearly layered from the side: front panel layer, main clamshell body, back/laptop layer — separated by zips running around the sides.
- FRONT IS CLEAN: one smooth panel with a faint satin sheen and soft fabric wrinkles. NO U-shaped pocket outline. A piped seam runs around the front panel's edge.
- A vertical zip runs down the viewer's LEFT edge of the front panel, from the top-left corner, with a long fabric pull tab at the top (quick-access pocket). The tech caddy opens with a zip across the top of the front panel.
- Lower band: a separate, slightly glossier (TPU-coated) panel across the bottom ~16–18% of the height. Its top seam is a soft curve (flat in the middle, rising toward the sides) and it wraps around the bottom corners and onto the sides and base.
- Logo: small lowercase "sympl studios" text, tone-on-tone, just above the lower-band seam, centred. A vertical "sympl studios" label patch on one side near the bottom.
- Top handle: thick, round, padded, glossy tube arch running left–right across the top, fairly tall (~6 cm clearance).
- Side zips: several vertical zip lines down each side with dark fabric pull tabs (clamshell, side access pocket).
- Bottle pocket: stretch black mesh sleeve on one side, covering the lower ~40–45% of the side, elastic top edge, slightly slanted, sits close to the body.
- Back: thick padded curved shoulder straps with "sympl studios" print, black webbing and plastic ladder buckles at the bottom corners; sternum strap with buckle; moulded back panel with channels; horizontal luggage pass-through band (striped webbing) on the lower back.
- Colour: near-black charcoal (around #1c1d1f) with a satin sheen. Not mid-grey.
